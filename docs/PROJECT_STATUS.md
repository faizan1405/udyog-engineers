- **Visual Identity:** Field-grade institutional aesthetic, strict Light Mode color palette (Forest Green `#1E3A2F`, Safety Gold `#D97706`, Slate Neutral `#111827`, Warm Industrial Gray `#F8FAFC`), hairline technical borders, micro-dot backgrounds.

- **Category Pages:** Full category matrix (`/categories`), dynamic category pages (`/categories/[slug]`) with specifications, equipment rosters, breadcrumb trails, and metadata.
- **Product Detail Architecture:** Dynamic product dossiers (`/products/[slug]`) featuring 4:3 technical gallery with zoom modal, interactive specifications table, equipment highlights, application badges, related instruments, and contextual inquiry modal integration.

- **Responsive QA:** Seamless multi-device behavior across mobile (375px/390px), tablet (768px/820px), desktop (1280px), and wide monitors (1536px+) with dedicated mobile drawer navigation and touch targets.
- **Motion System:** Fine-tuned Framer Motion choreography honoring `prefers-reduced-motion`, smooth micro-interactions, spring transitions, and non-distracting industrial entrance animations.
- **Design Consistency QA:** Systematic verification across all screens ensuring zero dark mode leakage, unified button & badge tokens, crisp borders, and consistent typography hierarchies.
